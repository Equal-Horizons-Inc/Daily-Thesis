Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Daily Thesis is built to keep your reading experience local and minimal wherever possible. This page explains what the app stores on your device, what it sends over the network, what third-party services it relies on, and how you can ask us to delete data we hold.

What Daily Thesis stores on your device only

The app stores the following on your device so it can personalize recommendations and remember your preferences. This app data is not uploaded as a reading profile.

  • Your name, if you enter it during onboarding or in Settings.
  • Your selected subjects and matched interest categories.
  • Your theme preference (System, Light, or Dark) and text size.
  • Papers you've liked, and papers you've skipped or marked "not interested in," so recommendations improve over time.
  • The local recommendation events and learned ranking information derived from those likes and dismissals.
  • Your paper history, so you can revisit papers you've previously been shown.
  • Papers you save to your Library.
  • Your reading streak and daily reminder settings.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of the above, since none of it is copied to a server.

Getting your daily paper

The app requests papers from its backend service, which retrieves research records from public scholarly sources. The request includes the selected subject, but the app does not attach your name, reading history, or an account identifier. Like any network request, it is sent from your device through your network connection; the service provider may process technical request information such as an IP address to deliver the response.

If you use “New to this field?” to request background context, the app sends the paper's title, abstract, paper identifier, and (when available) your selected field to a separate Cloudflare Worker. On a cache miss, that Worker sends the title, abstract, and field to OpenRouter using an AI model to generate a plain-language summary and key-term definitions. The generated context is cached by paper identifier for up to 180 days. This request does not include your name, account, or reading history. See OpenRouter's privacy information and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

When you tap "Read Full Publication," "Cite," or "Share," the app opens external links or system share sheets using your device's own apps and services.

Push notifications

The current app's “Daily reminder” is scheduled locally by your device. Its enabled state and chosen time stay on your device; the reminder does not require a push token or a server-side schedule. The supplied backend also contains an FCM registration and delivery capability. If remote push registration is enabled in a future app version, it will send a random install identifier, notification token, and selected subjects to the backend for notification delivery, and this policy will be revised to describe that live data flow.

The local reminder data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Remote push token handling is not currently called by this app build.

Analytics

The app enables Firebase Analytics when Firebase is configured and explicitly logs app opens. Firebase may also collect usage, diagnostic, and device information automatically as described in Google's documentation. Analytics may be unavailable on a device without valid Firebase configuration. Google processes analytics data under its own terms and privacy disclosures. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Advertising

Daily Thesis does not currently show ads. Ad support (Google AdMob) is built into the app but disabled. If we turn ads on in the future, this policy will be updated first to describe what that involves, and the change will also be reflected in the app's Play Store data safety disclosure.

Discuss with ChatGPT

If you tap “Discuss with ChatGPT,” the app opens ChatGPT with the paper's title, authors, and abstract pre-filled into a prompt for you to review and send. The app does not make this request to OpenAI; you choose whether to continue in ChatGPT. Use of ChatGPT is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

What Daily Thesis does not do

  • It does not require an account or login.
  • It does not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • It does not store your reading history, liked papers, saved papers, name, or full profile on a server — those stay on your device only.
  • It does not attach your name or local reading history to analytics. The current app build does not register remote push tokens with the supplied backend.

Requesting data deletion

The current app build does not register a remote push token with our backend. If you have used a future version that enables this feature, contact us at the address below with enough detail to locate the registration (for example, the approximate installation date) and request its deletion.

Locally stored data (name, history, liked papers, preferences) is entirely in your control — clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes this data immediately, without needing to contact us.

Children's privacy

Daily Thesis is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. The current version will always be published at this URL, with the "Last updated" date above reflecting the most recent revision.
